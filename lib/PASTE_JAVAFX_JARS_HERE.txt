PASTE YOUR JAVAFX .jar FILES IN THIS FOLDER.

Download JavaFX SDK from: https://gluonhq.com/products/javafx/
Extract and copy all .jar files from its lib/ folder into this folder.

Required jars (names may vary by version):
- javafx.base.jar
- javafx.controls.jar
- javafx.fxml.jar
- javafx.graphics.jar
- javafx.media.jar
- javafx.swing.jar
- javafx.web.jar
